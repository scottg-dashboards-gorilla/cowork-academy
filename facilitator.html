Facilitator kit

Everything you need to run a 90-minute live kickoff that gets a group using Claude on their own work, and leaving with ideas for Spark.

Before you run it for others, do the 1-hour AI Quick Start yourself so you've seen every screen.

Before the session

  • Every participant can sign in to Claude and open Cowork.
  • IT has confirmed which app connections (email, calendar, files) are allowed.
  • You have the company's AI policy, and a leader has agreed to open the session for 5 minutes.
  • Participants can sign in to Spark to submit ideas.
  • You've set up the starter folder yourself (the Set up Cowork mission walks you through it). It has every practice file the demo and missions use.
  • The invitation (template below) went out at least 3 days ahead.

The 90-minute kickoff

TimeWhat happens
0:00–0:10Why we're here. The leader opens: why AI matters to the company, permission to experiment, and that good ideas go into Spark and get built.
0:10–0:20Live demo. You run the demo script below on the practice data. Keep it fast and unrehearsed-looking: plain-English requests, real files.
0:20–0:30Safe habits. Everyone opens the AI Quick Start and takes the "Use Claude the right way" quiz. Talk through the three habits.
0:30–1:05Hands-on. Everyone does "Analyze real data and chart it" with the practice data from their starter folder. Walk the room. Call out first wins out loud.
1:05–1:25Spark your ideas. Everyone runs the ideas mission. Then go round the room: each person shares their best idea in one sentence.
1:25–1:30What's next. Finish a role path within a week, submit your idea in Spark, and put the office hours and showcase dates in calendars.

Live demo script

Start a Cowork task on your Cowork Academy folder, then paste these one at a time.

Open bookings.csv in this folder. Which channel brings in the most revenue per night, and which one has the most cancellations? Show me a chart.
Now look at maintenance-tickets.csv and guest-reviews.csv too. Which property has the most repeat problems, and do the reviews say the same thing?
Turn this into a one-page summary for leadership as a Word document, with 3 recommendations.

What to point out as it runs: it's plain English, the files are real spreadsheets, you check the numbers before trusting them, and Claude drafts while you decide. End with: "Imagine this arriving by itself every Monday."

Tips that make it work

  • They type, you don't. Hands on keyboards within the first 20 minutes.
  • Aim for a first win in 15 minutes. A chart from their own data changes the mood in the room.
  • Ask, don't fix. When a prompt goes wrong, ask "what did you want it to do?" and let them tell Claude.
  • Pair people who are stuck with someone a step ahead.
  • No real customer data during the session. Use the practice files.
  • Collect every idea. Answers saved in the Academy go into each person's idea notebook, ready to copy into Spark.

The 15-minute manager idea huddle

Run this with your team once a month. It's also a mission in the Leaders & Managers path.

TimeWhat happens
2 minRecap: one thing the team built or automated since last time.
8 minRound the table: "What did you try with Claude?" and "What's one thing you'd love to stop doing by hand?"
5 minPick the top one or two ideas, name who submits each in Spark, and celebrate the person with the best one.
