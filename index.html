🧭 New to Claude Cowork? Start here (2-min read)

What Cowork is: Claude Cowork is an AI teammate that doesn't just chat — it builds. It can create documents and spreadsheets, design websites, research the web, remember your projects, connect to your real apps, and even run work on a schedule. This course teaches you to do all of that, hands-on.

How this course works: each mission gives you a task and a ready-to-paste prompt. You do the task in your own Cowork window, then tick the boxes here. You learn by building real things — not by watching.

Two safety habits to carry from day one: (1) Cowork can draft emails and messages, but you press send. (2) It can help you spend or buy, but you confirm the money. Read, draft, and analyze freely; you approve anything with real-world consequences.

How it works

  1. Pick a session, a how-to guide or a level, then open the first mission.
  2. Copy the starter prompt and paste it into Claude Cowork (open in another window) to begin the task.
  3. Do the steps in Cowork, then tick each checkbox here as you finish it. The hints are optional tips to read if you get stuck — there's nothing to type into them.
  4. Everything you check or note saves automatically, so this page is your personal workbook and it'll be here when you come back.

Your checked steps are recorded for the program administrator (and cached in this browser); your notes stay on this device only. Switching computers or want a permanent copy? Use 📄 My Workbook (PDF) to save your whole record, or ⬇ Backup / ⬆ Restore to move it between devices.

How is my progress saved? (and is it private?)
You enter your name and email once to begin. From then on, your completed steps are recorded so the program administrator can see how everyone is progressing. Here's exactly how it works:

• What's recorded: your name, email, which steps you've checked off, and your last-active time. This is visible to the program administrator only — never to other learners.
• What stays on your device: your personal notes are saved only in this browser and are not uploaded.
• Also saved locally: your progress is cached in this browser too, so it loads instantly and still works offline (it syncs when you're back online).
• Portability: use 📄 My Workbook (PDF) for a permanent copy, or ⬇ Backup / ⬆ Restore to move your on-device record between machines.
• The things you actually build (websites, files, skills, connectors) live in your own Claude Cowork — not here. This page is your checklist; Cowork is your workshop.
• Proof of completion: finish a level to unlock a certificate you can share.
