    Three habits for using Claude at work

    1. Keep private things private. No passwords, and nothing your company's policy says stays inside approved tools.
    2. Claude drafts, you send. You approve anything that goes to a customer, spends money or changes a system.
    3. Check before you share. Look over numbers and facts before they leave your hands.
    New to Claude Cowork? What it is and how this works

    What Cowork is: Claude Cowork is an AI teammate that doesn't just chat; it does the work. It creates documents and spreadsheets, analyzes data, researches the web, connects to your apps and runs tasks on a schedule.

    How the Academy works: each mission gives you a ready-to-paste prompt. Do the task in your own Cowork window, then tick the steps here and answer the question about your own work. You learn by building real things.

    How is my progress saved, and is it private?
    What's recorded: your name, email, the steps you've checked, your capstones and your last-active time. Only your program administrator can see it, never other learners.

    What stays on your device: your notes and your idea notebook are saved only in this browser and are not uploaded.

    Moving devices: use Workbook PDF for a permanent copy, or Back up and Restore to move your record between computers.

    What you build (files, skills, connectors) lives in your own Claude Cowork, not here.
