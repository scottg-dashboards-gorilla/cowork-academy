How it works

Every path follows the same four steps. You work in Cowork in one window and follow the Academy in another. One step at a time, with a quick check so you know it worked.

  1. Set up

    Download one practice folder and point Cowork at it. Every file the missions need is already inside.

    About 10 minutes

  2. Learn by doing

    Each mission tells you exactly what to paste, what you should see, and what to do if you don't.

    10 to 30 minutes per mission

  3. Think about your work

    After each mission, jot down where it would save you time. Your answers collect in an idea notebook.

    One question per mission

  4. Get certified, spark an idea

    Pass the final check to get your certificate, then turn your best idea into a proposal your team can act on.

    80% to pass, retake any time

Questions

What do I need before I start?

The Claude desktop app on your computer, signed in with a Claude plan that includes Cowork (your company provides this). The Set up mission walks you through the rest in about 10 minutes.

Do I need to be technical?

No. Every step tells you what to paste, what you should see, and what to do if you don't. If you can write an email, you can do this.

How long does it take?

The Quick Start takes an hour, and Cowork at Work and the role paths take about two hours each. The full levels take half a day to two days. You can stop at any time; your progress is saved.

Is it safe to use with work information?

You practice on made-up data, so nothing real is at risk while you learn. The first mission covers the three habits for using Claude at work: keep private things private, Claude drafts and you send, and check before you share.

Who sees my progress?

Your name, email and completed steps are shared with your program administrator so they can support you. Your personal notes stay on your device. See the privacy notice.
