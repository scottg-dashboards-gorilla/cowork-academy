Privacy notice

What Cowork Academy collects, why, and the choices you have. Last updated November 1, 2026.

Draft. This notice is being reviewed by Datapath's legal counsel and may change.

Who we are

Cowork Academy is a hands-on training site for Claude Cowork, run by Datapath. "We" in this notice means Datapath.

What we collect

  • Who you are: your name, your email address, and an optional team or group name.
  • Your training progress: the steps you mark complete, the path you choose, the 10-minute guides you finish, and when you started and were last active.
  • What you choose to submit: capstone links or descriptions, and the 👍 / 👎 ratings you give missions.

We don't ask for, and you shouldn't enter, passwords, financial or government ID numbers, health information, or other sensitive personal information.

What stays on your device

Your notes, your answers to "Think about your work," and your idea notebook are saved only in your browser. They are not sent to us. A copy of your progress is also kept in your browser so the site works offline.

Why we use it

  • To save your progress and produce your certificates.
  • So Datapath's program administrators can see how learners are progressing and offer help.
  • If your employer arranged your training, to share your training progress with your employer's program contact.
  • To improve missions that learners find confusing.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your personal information or share it for advertising.
  • The site has no advertising, tracking pixels or analytics.
  • We don't use your information to make automated decisions about you.

Services we use

  • Supabase stores training records.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the website.
  • Google Fonts provides the typefaces, so your browser requests font files from Google.

What you create in Claude Cowork itself is covered by your own or your employer's agreement with Anthropic, not by this notice.

How long we keep it

We keep training records while your training program is active, and delete them when you ask us to or when the program ends.

Your choices and rights

  • See and download your record with "Workbook PDF" and "Back up" on the home page, or ask us for a copy.
  • Correct your details by asking us.
  • Delete your progress with "Reset" on the home page. To delete your name and email as well, contact us.

If you live in California, you have the right to know what personal information we collect, to have it deleted or corrected, to opt out of its sale or sharing (we don't sell or share it), and to limit the use of sensitive personal information (we don't collect any). We won't treat you differently for using these rights.

Contact

For questions or requests, email scottg@mydatapath.com.

Changes

If we change this notice, we'll update the date at the top.
