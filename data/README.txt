Cowork Academy practice data pack
=================================
Fictional data for a made-up vacation-rental management company with 12 properties, April to September 2026.
None of it is real: properties, guests, vendors and numbers are invented for practice.
Use these files in Cowork whenever a mission asks for data and you don't want to use your own.

bookings.csv             One row per booking: property, area, channel, dates, nights, rate, fees, revenue, status.
guest-reviews.csv        Guest reviews linked to bookings: date, rating (1-5) and comment.
maintenance-tickets.csv  Maintenance tickets: property, dates, category, issue, priority, vendor, cost, status.

There are real patterns hidden in the data. See what Cowork finds.
