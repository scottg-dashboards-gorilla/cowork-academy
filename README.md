# 🎓 Cowork Academy

A self-paced, leveled course for learning **Claude Cowork** by building a real website — using Cowork's built-in skills and connecting it to real tools.

**▶️ Live site: https://scottg-dashboards-gorilla.github.io/cowork-academy/**

## Pick your level
- 🌱 **Beginner — Your First Site:** a one-page website, your first built-in skill, one read-only connector. Step-by-step.
- ⚡ **Intermediate — The Smart Brochure:** a multi-section business site, your own saved custom skill, a connector feeding live data in.
- 🔥 **Higher Intermediate — The Connected Product:** a multi-page site, an agent, a custom skill, two connectors working together, and a live dashboard. Open challenges.

## How it works
Each mission gives you a task to do in **Claude Cowork** (open in another window). You check off steps and jot notes in the app as you go. **Everything saves automatically in your own browser** — so the page is your private workbook, and it's there when you come back. Use the **Backup** / **Restore** buttons to move progress between devices.

No login, no accounts — each person who opens the link gets their own private instance on their device.
